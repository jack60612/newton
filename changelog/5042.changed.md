`SolverMuJoCo(deterministic=wp.DeterministicMode.RUN_TO_RUN)` on the MuJoCo Warp backend now selects MuJoCo Warp's own deterministic option (`Model.opt.deterministic = DeterminismType.ALL`: canonical contact and constraint row order and deterministic atomic reductions with record bounds MuJoCo Warp sizes itself) instead of compiling MuJoCo Warp's modules in Warp's deterministic mode; MuJoCo Warp's modules and lazily created kernels never inherit Warp's mode, and Newton's contact conversion kernels keep the requested Warp mode. `GPU_TO_GPU` raises `ValueError` on the MuJoCo Warp backend.
